Safe practical experiment: toy car and ramp

  1. Use a small toy car and a low, stable ramp.
  2. Mark a measured distance on a clear, level surface.
  3. Release the car without pushing it and time its travel.
  4. Repeat several trials and calculate average speed.
  5. Compare the results and discuss timing error and friction.

Keep the ramp low and the area clear. Do not use heavy objects or dangerous equipment.
